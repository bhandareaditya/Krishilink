Why KrishiLink

Most farmers sell right after harvest, when prices dip. KrishiLink helps them hold, borrow and sell at the right time.

An illustrative season

Harvest week

Everyone sells at once. Mandis fill up and prices often fall to their lowest of the season.

Store and borrow

Keep grain in a WDRA-registered warehouse, get an e-NWR receipt, and borrow against it from a bank.

Sell on recovery

Sell when the price is right. Repay the loan from the sale and keep the difference after rent and interest.
